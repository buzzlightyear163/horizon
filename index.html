    Questions

    Before you ask.

    Does an escape mean a coin is good?

    No. Escaping only means the coin filled its bonding curve and moved to a DEX pool. Plenty of escaped coins fall later. HORIZON observes. It never says buy.

    Where does the data come from?

    New launches and escapes come from PumpPortal’s free public stream, straight to your browser. The checker asks DexScreener’s public API. No keys, no accounts, nothing stored.

    Is the counter the real graduation rate?

    It counts escapes per launch while this page is open. Coins escaping now mostly launched earlier, so the first minutes are noisy. Leave it open for an hour and it settles. The real rate moves a lot: this year it has gone from well under 1% to over 6% in a single day.

    How do you know how close $HORIZON is to escaping?

    HORIZON reads $HORIZON’s price on its bonding curve from DexScreener every 15 seconds and works out how much of the curve is filled. That percentage is an estimate. The escape itself comes from pump.fun’s own migration event, the moment it happens.

    What does Preview mean?

    If the live stream can’t connect, for example inside some embeds or networks, the disk runs on simulated launches and the lamp says Preview. Simulated numbers are labelled, never shown as real, and share cards say so.

    Why does the checker say not found?

    DexScreener can take a minute to list a brand-new coin, and some coins never get listed. Try again shortly, and check that the address is complete.

    Will HORIZON DM me?

    Never. There is no presale, no airdrop and no support account. Anyone messaging you as HORIZON is not us.
